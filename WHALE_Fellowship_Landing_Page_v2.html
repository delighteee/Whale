Is this you?

WHALE is built for
a specific kind of person.

You belong here if —
  • You are a student or graduate who wants real women's health experience, not another certificate on the shelf
  • You want something on your CV that actually took effort and can be discussed in detail at an interview
  • You are building toward public health, global health, advocacy, research, or NGO work
  • You are willing to show up weekly, do assignments, run outreach, and be accountable to a team
  • You want to be part of building a Pan-African movement, not just learning about one
This is not for you if —
  • You want to consume content at your own pace with no accountability
  • You are looking for general clinical or medical knowledge
  • You want the certificate without the work that earns it
  • Group projects, fieldwork, and weekly commitments don't fit where you are right now
  • You're shopping for the cheapest certificate, not six months of work

How the fellowship runs

Learning and doing
from day one.

Step 1

Learn

Weekly live sessions on Women's health, research and programme skills, taught by the industry's best!

Step 2

Cluster

You are placed in a team from Week 1. Your cluster is your accountability system, your research team, your outreach unit, and your most durable professional network.

Step 3

Go to the Field

By Week 5, your cluster is facilitating sessions with real adolescent girls through the Gynix Girls' Club. You plan it, execute it, and track what changes over months.

Step 4

Research

Your fieldwork becomes your capstone. Same participants. Same data. One body of work that is both a programme and a study — yours to publish.

Step 5

Graduate

Certificate. Reference letter. Research report. Field portfolio. Alumni network. Six months of proof — not of attendance, of work.

FAQ

We answered some questions
so you don't have to ask.

Is it online or in person?

Live sessions are online, so you can join from wherever you are. Fieldwork is in person — you facilitate with real adolescent girls, not a Zoom role-play.

Where does the fieldwork run?

Through the Gynix Girls' Club, in communities Gynix Afrika already works in. Your cluster is placed on a real programme. We'll match you when you're accepted.

How many spots are there?

This is the first cohort. We keep it small enough that every fellow is in a cluster and actually supervised — we don't publish a fake number. Apply by 20 September. If the cohort is full, we'll say so.

Who can apply?

Students and graduates building toward public health, global health, advocacy, research, or NGO work. If you want real experience — not another certificate — this is built for you.

How much time does it take?

Six months, starting October 2026. Weekly live sessions, plus fieldwork with your cluster from week 5.

What do I actually leave with?

A research report with your name on it, a programme you ran, a certificate that names the work, a guaranteed reference letter, and a cluster that lasts.
